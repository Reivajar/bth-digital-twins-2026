What this does not show

  • Heights are measured for only part of the buildings. The rest are estimated from floor counts or set to a default of 6 m (click a building to see which).
  • Ground elevation is approximate (30 m grid, includes some buildings and trees).
  • The "model last checked" year is invented for teaching. No public source records it.

Data: Overture Maps Foundation (buildings, water, land use; includes OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL) and Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (© DLR, Airbus, provided under COPERNICUS by the EU and ESA). See docs/DATA.md.
